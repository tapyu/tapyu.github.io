Rubem Vasconcelos Pacelli

GNSS, AI in comm./nav., deep learning, signal processing, estimation, detection & adaptive filtering.

He holds a B.Sc. (2013–2018) in Electronics Engineering from the University of Fortaleza, and an M.Sc. (2018–2021) and a Ph.D. (2021–2026) in Teleinformatics Engineering from the Federal University of Ceará (UFC), as well as a Ph.D. (2023–2026) in Aerospace Science and Technology from the Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya (UPC, Spain), under a cotutelle agreement.

From 2014 to 2021, he worked on satellite communication systems with INPE (Brazil's National Institute for Space Research, Eusébio unit), and has since kept a continuous focus on satellite navigation and communication (SatNavCom). His work centers on the signal processing chain, with interest in the following applications: implementation of digital modems in software-defined radios using FPGAs; carrier synchronization; monitoring and mitigation of ionospheric scintillation in GNSS receivers; and radar and remote sensing.

Since 2026, he has been affiliated with INCT Signals: Signal Processing, Communications, Sensing and Surveillance. He is currently a postdoctoral researcher at UFC and at the Aeronautics Institute of Technology (ITA, São José dos Campos, São Paulo). He has conducted high-impact research in SatNavCom using signal processing and artificial intelligence algorithms, addressing the following topics: statistical signal processing; deep machine learning; system identification; estimation, detection, and optimization theory; pattern recognition; and adaptive filtering.
